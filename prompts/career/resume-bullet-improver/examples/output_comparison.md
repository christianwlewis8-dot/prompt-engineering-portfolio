### Prompt

Improve this resume bullet: Detailed cars for customers.

- **Evaluator Score:** 25/100

### Raw Output

- **Focus on Quality & Precision:** Executed full-service interior and exterior automotive detailing—including paint correction, polishing, and deep upholstery cleaning—restoring [Number]+ vehicles to showroom condition.
- **Focus on Volume & Efficiency:** Processed and detailed an average of [Number] customer vehicles weekly in a fast-paced environment while consistently meeting tight turnaround deadlines.
- **Focus on Customer Satisfaction:** Delivered customized vehicle detailing services tailored to client preferences, maintaining a [X]% satisfaction rating and driving repeat business.
- **Concise & Action-Oriented:** Applied professional detailing techniques, specialized equipment, and premium chemical treatments to clean, sanitize, and protect customer vehicles.

**How to customize these further:**

- **Add tools and techniques:** Mention specific tools or methods used, such as rotary polishers, ceramic coatings, steam extractors, or leather-restoration products.
- **Quantify the work:** Fill in the bracketed placeholders with specific numbers, such as vehicles detailed per week, team size, or customer-review scores.

## Designed Prompt Run (C-A-R-E)

### Prompt

Context: I am a high school student creating a resume for a part-time job. I run a small car-detailing business where I clean vehicle interiors and exteriors, communicate with customers, schedule appointments, and complete jobs independently. My original resume bullet is: "Detailed cars for customers."

Action: Rewrite this into a stronger resume bullet that clearly explains my responsibilities and skills. Use a strong action verb and focus on skills that would matter to an employer. Do not make up numbers, customer ratings, or services that I did not provide.

Result: Give me three different resume-bullet options. Keep each option to one sentence, use a professional but natural tone, and keep each one under 30 words.

Example: A weak bullet such as "Mowed lawns for people" could become "Managed recurring lawn-care jobs for local customers while maintaining dependable scheduling, clear communication, and consistent work quality."

### Score

- **Evaluator Score:** 100/100

### Raw Output

- Managed an independent vehicle-detailing business, providing comprehensive interior and exterior cleaning while handling client scheduling and direct communication.
- Delivered full-service interior and exterior vehicle detailing independently, maintaining high quality standards, reliable appointment scheduling, and clear customer communication.
- Executed end-to-end car detailing projects for local clients, combining thorough cleaning services with dependable schedule management and prompt communication.
