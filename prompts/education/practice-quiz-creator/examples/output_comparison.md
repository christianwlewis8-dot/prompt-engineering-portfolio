### Prompt

Format: The quiz should be multiple choice with exactly four answer choices for each question. Include a complete answer key at the end.

### Score

- **Evaluator Score:** 100/100

### Raw Output

Welcome 5th graders! Test your water-cycle knowledge with this quick three-question practice quiz!

### Practice Quiz: The Water Cycle

1. Imagine you leave a glass of water outside on a hot summer day. A few hours later, some of the water is gone! What part of the water cycle caused the liquid water to turn into invisible water vapor?  
   A) Precipitation  
   B) Evaporation  
   C) Condensation  
   D) Freezing

2. As water vapor rises high into the sky, it cools down and turns back into tiny drops of liquid water that bunch together. What is this process that forms clouds?  
   A) Condensation  
   B) Evaporation  
   C) Collection  
   D) Melting

3. When clouds get heavy and full of water drops, gravity pulls the water back down to Earth. Which option lists forms of precipitation?  
   A) Sunlight, wind, and clouds  
   B) Rain, snow, sleet, and hail  
   C) Oceans, rivers, and lakes  
   D) Steam, smoke, and fog

### Answer Sheet & Explanations

1. **B) Evaporation** - Heat from the sun turns liquid water into invisible gas called water vapor.
2. **A) Condensation** - Cool temperatures high up cause water vapor to turn back into liquid drops, forming clouds.
3. **B) Rain, snow, sleet, and hail** - Precipitation is any form of water that falls from clouds down to Earth!
