# Frameworks

## Structures

- **RTF:** Role, Task, Format. Used for the quiz prompt.
- **Modified RTF:** Role, Context, Task, Format. Context adds business details. Used for the business prompt.
- **CARE:** Context, Action, Result, Example. Used for the resume prompt.

## Techniques

- **Zero shot:** Give instructions without an example. Used for the quiz prompt.
- **Few shot:** Give examples for the AI to follow. Used for the resume prompt.
- **Zero shot chain of thought:** Ask the AI to think through the task step by step without a worked example. Used for the business prompt.

## Scoring

Clarity and specificity: 30 points. Structure and framework use: 30 points. Context and inputs: 20 points. Output requirements: 20 points. Total: 100 points.
